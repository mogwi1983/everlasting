# The Everlasting Delve — Player Guide

## Start a campaign

Choose **Begin a New Campaign**, enter a hero name, then select one of four race-and-class combinations: human fighter, elf ranger, dwarf cleric, or halfling rogue. If a saved campaign already exists, the game asks before replacing it. Choose **Continue** to return to that campaign.

## Take a turn

Read the current location and the Dungeon Master's narration. Pick one of her suggested actions, or type your own action and send it. Each choice advances the scene. The rules resolve the intent with a character-stat modifier and a d20-style roll; failed checks change the consequence but do not end the campaign.

The Dungeon Master is a local, rules-based narrative engine rather than a live generative-AI service. It keeps your exact custom-action text, classifies its intent (for example: talk, track, investigate, fight, help, rest, trade, or loot), and adapts the authored response and game consequences to that intent. No network account or AI service is required.

## Quests, experience and gear

Complete three actions in a quest to receive its reward and reveal the next adventure. The first cycle contains five authored locations. After that, the campaign continues in numbered Chronicles with the same five-quest cycle; it has no final victory screen, although its authored quest set is finite and repeats.

Actions and completed quests award experience, levels, gold and occasional finds. You can also enter a custom action to loot a cache, or buy supplies from the traveling shop. Items stay in your campaign inventory; use a herbal poultice from the character panel to recover health.

## Saving

The game saves campaign progress to local game storage in the browser profile. It is not cloud-synced; clearing the browser's site data may remove the save.
